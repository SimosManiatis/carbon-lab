# Carbon Lab

Parametric low-carbon building concept for the [Carbon Lab](https://ehv26hack.tech/themes/carbon-lab) challenge at the Knoop XL Eindhoven hackathon (13 November 2026).

The question: for one representative Fellenoord plot, which floor should the structure switch from a concrete podium to timber, once district energy (WKO) and summer comfort are counted together?

## Working idea

- One stepped mixed-use block, podium plus housing.
- Live control: the switch floor between concrete and timber.
- Compare upfront carbon, a summer comfort check, and two energy futures (WKO-connected vs standalone heat pump) against an all-concrete baseline.
- Disassembly: concrete cores stay; timber floor cassettes are bolted and recorded in a material passport.

Calculation boundaries stay visible: upfront only (A1–A5, no biogenic credit), whole life, and whole life with reuse.
